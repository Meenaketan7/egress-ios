Case Study · Real Incident
Station Platform Crowd Surge
Recreation · approximate · educational

On a crowded evening, arriving passengers met a platform already at capacity. A narrowing near the single stairwell reduced outward flow below the rate people entered.

Density rose faster than the platform could clear. Investigators attributed the outcome to the geometry of the exit route, not the behaviour of those present.

This recreation illustrates how flow capacity, not panic, governs a surge.

Sourced from public inquiry summaries · presented for education, without blame or casualty imagery.

PROPOSAL
Preparedness · Reading
Why corridors decide everything

Evacuation is rarely limited by how fast people walk. It is limited by the narrowest point they must pass through together.

A corridor that loses half a metre can double the time a room takes to clear — because flow is set by width, not effort.

The assembly-corridor minimum of 1.2 m exists for exactly this reason.

Warm paper ground, serif body — the reading tabs only. The Sim Canvas never adopts it.
